Waitlist privacy policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This policy covers the Pure Prep waitlist at pureprep.app/waitlist. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Your email address (required)
  • Your first name, if you give it
  • What matters most to you in a meal plan (optional ranking)
  • Whether you’d like to help test the early Alpha version, if you answer

This page doesn’t use cookies, analytics, or tracking pixels.

Why we collect it

To email you launch updates about Pure Prep and, if you said yes, to invite you to test the early Alpha. Your ranking helps us understand what people want from the app. We’ll only email you about Pure Prep, and you can unsubscribe anytime.

Where it’s stored

Sign-ups are submitted to a Google Form and stored in Google Forms and Google Sheets, under Google’s privacy policy. Only the Pure Prep team can see the responses. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

No selling or sharing

We don’t sell, rent, or share your information with anyone. We keep it until you ask us to remove it or we close the waitlist.

How to be removed

To be removed from the waitlist or to ask what we have on file, reply to any Pure Prep email or send a DM to @purepreppapp on Instagram. We’ll delete your entry.

Not medical advice

Pure Prep offers general meal-planning information. It is not medical or nutritional advice and isn’t a substitute for a doctor or registered dietitian. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a history of disordered eating, talk to a professional before changing how you eat.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the effective date above.
