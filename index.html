How it works

A few minutes of setup, then a plan you can actually prep.

  1. Set your targets

    Enter your calories and macros, or let Pure Prep estimate them from a few quick questions.

  2. Pick your meals

    Choose from savory bowls, yogurt bowls, smoothies, and oatmeal or overnight oats.

  3. Prep and eat

    Follow the prep steps, grab the grocery list if you want it, and let your menu rotate.
